Too much food is wasted. Too many people need it.

Canteens, restaurants and events often finish the day with safe, edible food and no way to move it in time.

Collection windows are short. Coordination happens over scattered phone calls. Trusted partners are hard to find.

FoodLoop gives donors one place to list surplus and recipients one place to claim it.

Trust and safety

  • Verified donors. Recognised donors carry a shield badge after an admin reviews them.
  • Your responsibility. Donors confirm food is safe. FoodLoop coordinates pickups; it does not certify any food item.
  • Report a listing. Use the report button on any listing if something looks wrong.
  • Privacy. We only ask for what is needed to arrange a pickup. Demo data stays in your browser.

    How we estimate

    Servings are the number the donor entered. Food weight is estimated at 0.4 kg per serving. We count a pickup only after the recipient confirms collection. These are estimates and not a measure of waste prevented.
